## 1. Executive Summary

Jobiest (`jobiest.com`) is an AI-powered career and recruitment technology company founded in Africa with a disciplined 10-year mandate: **make finding verified, high-fit roles faster and more truthful for candidates while making discovering, evaluating, and hiring global talent dramatically simpler for employers.**

Unlike generic AI resume generators or opaque mass-application bots, Jobiest operates on three foundational product truths:
1. **Verified Candidate Readiness:** 30 distinct, industry-calibrated ISO A4 resume and CV templates in Resume Studio, backed by honest ATS layout guidance, 10 free career tools, public candidate portfolios, and structured career profiles.
2. **Truthful, Human-Supervised Application Execution:** Approval-first tailored CV and cover letter preparation against real job descriptions, with monthly quotas counting **only confirmed, non-duplicate submitted applications** (never failed or retried attempts).
3. **Structured Two-Sided Talent Infrastructure:** A searchable, opt-in Talent Pool paired with employer discovery, curated candidate shortlists, recruitment partnerships, and enterprise hiring workflows.

## 9. Eight Non-Negotiable Business Rules

1. **Never Count an Outbound Email as a Partnership:** Only partnerships with documented proof (signed MoU/pilot, created employer account, shared active vacancy, agency agreement, or written decision-maker confirmation) count on the Scorecard.
2. **Never Inflate MRR with Manual Grants:** Complimentary `ADMIN_GRANT` subscriptions are strictly separated from verified payment receipts (`Paystack`, `Flutterwave`, `Dodo`) in all internal and investor reporting.
3. **Never Charge Users for Failed Applications:** Monthly application allowances (`30` Basic, `50` Premium, `Unlimited*` Ultra) count **only** confirmed, non-duplicate `SUBMITTED` applications.
4. **Candidate Truthfulness Over Fabrication:** Jobiest tailors real candidate experience and skills; it never invents degrees, employers, or credentials, and keeps human approval on by default.
5. **Respect Platform Terms, Security & Privacy:** No CAPTCHA bypassing, credential scraping, or unauthorized third-party job-board automation; strict RLS, TOTP 2FA, and GDPR/NDPA compliance.
6. **Radical Stage & Compensation Honesty:** Every intern, volunteer, freelancer, employee, and partner receives transparent written terms and a 60-day review without false equity or salary promises.
7. **Supply Quality Before Supply Vanity:** A smaller pool of verified, interview-ready candidates beats 100,000 incomplete signups; employers renew because shortlists convert to hires.
8. **Unit-Economic Discipline:** AI inference, email, and infrastructure spend must remain metered per tier so gross margin stays above `70%` at every stage of scale.

## 11. Founder's Four Core Priorities & Three Strategic Decisions Framework

### The Founder's Four Core Priorities
1. **Narrative, Visibility & Trust:** Represent Jobiest publicly across LinkedIn, media, universities, and employer conversations so top builders and hiring managers trust the brand.
2. **US & UK Employer Relationships:** Personally lead discovery calls and pilot design for the first **50 US & UK company partnerships** until the B2B sales playbook is repeatable.
3. **Product Truth & Quality Bar:** Guard the candidate and employer experience—ensuring Resume Studio, tailored documents, and Talent Pool shortlists remain unmistakably superior.
4. **Capital Allocation & Talent Density:** Recruit high-ownership builders through `jobiest.com/apply`, enforce the 60-day contribution review, and allocate every dollar/naira to the constraint that moves the current Scorecard milestone.

### The Three Strategic Decisions Framework (Used at Every Quarterly Gate)
Whenever evaluating a new feature, partnership, market, or hire, leadership applies three sequential filter questions:
1. **Decision 1 - Focus vs. Breadth (Does it move the active Milestone Scorecard within 90 days?):** Before 31 March 2027, if an initiative does not directly add structured Talent Pool members, verified paying subscribers, or documented US/UK partnerships, it waits.
2. **Decision 2 - Product-Led Self-Serve vs. High-Touch Concierge:** Use high-touch founder/BD concierge execution first to win and understand US/UK employer pilots (2026-2027), then codify only proven workflows into self-serve SaaS and AI automation (2028+).
3. **Decision 3 - Local Cash Flow vs. Global Hard-Currency Leverage:** Maintain accessible NGN pricing (`₦5K / ₦10K / ₦20K`) to dominate African candidate adoption and volume while pricing B2B recruitment, diaspora subscriptions, and enterprise software in **USD/GBP** to build hard-currency resilience and venture-scale enterprise value.
